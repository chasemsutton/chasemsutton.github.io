# Super Troops verification

Normal self-training and boosting caps, checked Nov 3, 2026. Super Troops remain hidden on the home screen.

Each normal Super Troop level matches the same base troop level. The required TH is the maximum of TH11, the base troop unlock, and the TH needed for its Laboratory research. Boost unlock also requires the minimum base troop level shown on the Super Troop page. Starred event-only levels are retained separately and excluded from normal unlocks. Clan Castle donations and temporary boosts do not determine these caps.

## Evidence

Every item in super.json records the Super Troop, base troop, Laboratory, unlock-building, and rule-template revision IDs plus raw-wikitext hashes. Base research costs and times are preserved per level, without discounts. Super Hog Rider and Super Miner pages include their October 20, 2026 HP adjustments; these were cross-checked against [Supercell’s October notes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/balance-changes-incoming-october-2026/).

## Special levels

- Super Wall Breaker: 5*, 6* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
- Rocket Balloon: 5*, 6*, 7* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
- Super Dragon: 3*, 4*, 5*, 6* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
- Super Yeti: 1*, 2* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
- Super Minion: 4*, 5*, 6*, 7* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
- Super Witch: 1*, 2*, 3*, 4* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
- Ice Hound: 1*, 2*, 3*, 4* are event-only, not normal unlocks.
